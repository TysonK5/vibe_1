How to play

Answer five questions about the fictional Riverbend Reading Club. Each question has three choices. You get one point for a correct answer and zero for a wrong one.

  • Start begins question 1 with a score of zero.
  • Choose one answer. That question then locks; a second click does not change the score.
  • Read the short explanation, then use Next.
  • After question 5, Next shows your score out of 5.
  • Play again returns to question 1 with zero points.

This is a classroom exercise. The club, gathering, and website it describes are fictional.
