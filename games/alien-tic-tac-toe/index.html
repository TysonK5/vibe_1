How to play

This is a solo board. You pick every square. A short alien battle decides whether that square becomes X (you) or O (the alien).

  • Choose an empty square. The board locks until that battle ends.
  • Both sides start with 2 health. One response is accepted per exchange.
  • Correct response: the alien loses 1 health. Wrong response: you lose 1 health.
  • Three X in a line wins the board. Three O in a line loses. A full board with no line is a draw.
  • The alien does not take a second square after a battle. Restart game clears the whole board.
