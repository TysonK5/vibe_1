How to play

The bird falls. Each tap, click, or Space press is one flap. Holding does not keep you up. Fly through the gaps in the green pipes.

  • Start shows the bird waiting. The first flap begins the round.
  • Each pipe you pass awards one point, once.
  • Hitting a pipe or the ground ends the round.
  • Play again resets the bird, pipes, score, and movement.
